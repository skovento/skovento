# Blog ane Case Study ni suggestion (Skovento)

## Blogs — kevi type na lakhvi

Goal: SEO + "aa loko ne khabar chhe" evo trust. Author nu naam jaruri nathi — company-first lakho ("Skovento Team").

**Lakhva jevi (high value):**
1. **Decision guides** — "Custom software vs off-the-shelf", "Shopify vs WooCommerce vs custom", "PWA vs mobile app: kyu pasand karvu?" (buyer ne decision ma madad kare)
2. **Industry problem → solution** — "Clinic ma no-show kem ghatadva", "Retail ma stock mismatch kem rokvu" (Industries pages sathe link karo)
3. **How-it-works / technical explainers** — "Core Web Vitals shu chhe", "Headless CMS shu chhe", "RAG chatbot kem kaam kare" (simple bhasha ma)
4. **Process & transparency** — "Project estimate kem banay chhe", "Discovery phase ma shu thay chhe", "Handover checklist"
5. **Checklists / templates** — "Website launch SEO checklist", "CRM requirement checklist" (link magnet)

**Na lakhvi:**
- Khota stats / "100% guaranteed ranking" jevi claims
- Competitor ni burai ke compare with names
- Generic AI-style "Top 10 trends" jema koi depth nathi
- Copy-paste content (Google penalise kare)
- Koi client no naam / data permission vagar

**Rules:** 800–1500 shabdo, ek topic = ek keyword, 2–3 internal links (service + industry), ek clear CTA, real example/number j (je tame verify kari sako).
**Cadence:** shru ma 8–10 deep articles, pachhi mahine 2.

## Case Studies — kevi rite

Structure (page ma already chhe): Challenge → Solution → Outcomes → Tech.

- **Real result j** — metric tame verify kari sako to j lakho. Na hoy to "Illustrative sample" label rakho (site ma auto dekhay chhe jyare `isSample` ON hoy).
- **Client no naam chhupavo hoy** to "A multi-doctor clinic" jevu anonymised lakho.
- **Client permission** vagar logo / naam / screenshot nahi.
- Fake testimonial, fake logo, fake number kyarey nahi (trust tuti jay, legal risk pan).
- Har case study ne ek **service** ane ek **industry** sathe link karo (admin ma relation select karo) — internal linking SEO mate.
