## ✨ Animations & Visual Enhancements (Inspired by Manhar Creatives)

1. **Luxury Typography Setup**: Use `Clash Display` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` for high-impact geometric titles paired with `Satoshi` / `Inter` for sleek body copy.
2. **Animated Gradient Text**: Shifting gradient on headlines ("Build. Launch. Grow.") cycling through Royal Indigo (`#1E2E6B`) and Violet Magenta (`#8A2B88`).
3. **Smooth Inertial Scroll (Lenis + Framer Motion)**: Buttery-smooth page scrolling with soft spring physics.
4. **Pulsing Floating Consultation Widget**: A persistent floating action button in the bottom right with a subtle glowing pulse ring (`#C88770` Rose Gold accent) for instant strategy call bookings.
5. **Magnetic Button Hover Effect**: Primary buttons magnetically track cursor position on hover for a tactile luxury feel.
6. **3D Tilt & Glassmorphism Cards**: Hover depth tilt on 12 service cards and case studies with glowing inner borders (`#EAE4DC`).
7. **Viewport Count-Up Stats**: Numbers like `50+`, `180%`, `3x`, `98%` smoothly count up as they scroll into view.
